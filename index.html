How to Spot a Scam SMS

▼
  • 1
    Look for extreme urgency: Scammers say "Act within 10 minutes or your account will be closed!" Legitimate companies give you days or weeks.
  • 2
    Beware of unexpected free gifts: If you never bought a lottery ticket or entered a competition, you did not win cash or a car.
  • 3
    Never click strange internet links: Official banks communicate through their verified mobile app or official SMS shortcode, not random links like bit.ly/bank99.
  • 4
    Never pay upfront fees to claim money: Asking for "clearance fee", "registration tax", or "processing fee" before giving you prize money is always a trap.
  • 5
    Show it to a trusted person first: If you feel confused or rushed, stop and show the message to a family member or friend before touching anything.
✉️

How to Create Your First Email

▼
  • 1
    Email is like a private digital mailbox: It belongs solely to you and allows you to receive job offers, government notices, and receipts.
  • 2
    Choose a trusted free service: Download the official Google Gmail or Microsoft Outlook app directly from your phone's official app store.
  • 3
    Select a clean, professional username: Use your actual name or initials (e.g., amina.kamau24@gmail.com instead of superstar99@gmail.com).
  • 4
    Create a strong, memorable secret password: Combine 3 familiar words that you can recall (e.g., GreenMangoTree2026!). Never share it with anyone.
  • 5
    Write your login details in a private paper notebook: Keep your login written down in a safe notebook at home so you never lose access.
🛡️

How to Stay Safe on WhatsApp

▼
  • 1
    Turn on 2-Step Verification: Open WhatsApp Settings > Account > Two-step verification. Set a 6-digit PIN to prevent thieves from hijacking your account.
  • 2
    Never share your 6-digit SMS code: If a contact messages saying "I accidentally sent my verification code to your phone, please send it back!", it is an attacker.
  • 3
    Control group permissions: In Settings > Privacy > Groups, change permissions from 'Everyone' to 'My Contacts' so random people cannot add you to scam groups.
  • 4
    Beware of forwarded messages: Messages tagged "Forwarded many times" often spread falsehoods, false investment schemes, and medical hoaxes.
  • 5
    Block and report immediately: If an unknown number asks for cash or sends inappropriate content, tap their contact name and select 'Block & Report'.
💳

What is Mobile Money & How to Use It

  • 1
    Mobile money is cash stored on your phone SIM: Systems like M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, or UPI turn your phone number into a secure digital pocket.
  • 2
    Your PIN is the key to your home: Never tell anyone your secret 4-digit or 6-digit PIN. Real customer support agents will never ask for it.
  • 3
    Verify the recipient's name carefully: Before approving any transfer, read the confirmation prompt to ensure the name matches your intended recipient.
  • 4
    Beware of fake payment confirmation SMS: Fraudsters send an ordinary SMS that mimics a money deposit, then call saying "I sent money by mistake, please return it!" Always check your actual balance first.
  • 5
    Guard your screen in public: When entering your PIN at a kiosk or market stall, shield your keypad so nobody records your numbers.
💼

How to Search for a Job Online

  • 1
    Real employers NEVER make you pay to work: If a job demands an "interview fee", "medical check deposit", or "uniform registration fee", it is a fraud.
  • 2
    Use verified job platforms: Look for opportunities on official company websites or legitimate boards like LinkedIn, Indeed, or national portals.
  • 3
    Prepare a simple 1-page CV: List your phone number, email, skills, and past work or trade experience clearly and honestly.
  • 4
    Protect sensitive identification papers: Do not upload pictures of your national passport, identity card, or bank account before a legitimate in-person interview.
  • 5
    Confirm the physical interview location: Make sure interviews happen at an established public commercial office, and notify a relative before attending.
